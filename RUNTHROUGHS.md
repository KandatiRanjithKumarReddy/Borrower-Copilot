## 👩 Case 1: Priya — Salaried Software Engineer in Bengaluru

### The Story:
Priya is 29 years old and works at a large tech MNC in Bengaluru. She takes home ₹1,10,000 every month after all tax deductions. She has a great CIBIL score of 780 and has ₹3,00,000 set aside in emergency savings. She is getting married soon and wants to borrow ₹8,00,000 as a personal loan for 3 years to help cover wedding expenses.

---

### Questions Asked & Answers Given

#### Step 1: Profile & Income
- **Question:** How old are you?  
  **Answer:** 29 years
- **Question:** Which city do you live in?  
  **Answer:** Bengaluru
- **Question:** What is your work type?  
  **Answer:** Salaried
- **Question:** What is your net take-home salary each month?  
  **Answer:** ₹1,10,000
- **Question:** How stable is your monthly income?  
  **Answer:** Stable (fixed monthly salary credit)

#### Step 2: Loan Requirements
- **Question:** What is the primary purpose of this loan?  
  **Answer:** Wedding expenses
- **Question:** Which loan type are you applying for?  
  **Answer:** Personal Loan (unsecured)
- **Question:** How much money do you want to borrow?  
  **Answer:** ₹8,00,000 (₹8 Lakh)
- **Question:** Over how many years do you want to repay?  
  **Answer:** 3 Years (36 monthly installments)

#### Step 3: Current Monthly Expenses & Loans
- **Question:** How much do you currently pay each month for existing loans?  
  **Answer:** ₹14,000 (ongoing car/gadget EMI)
- **Question:** What are your monthly household living expenses (food, bills, utilities)?  
  **Answer:** ₹20,000
- **Question:** How much do you pay in house rent each month?

#### Step 4: Work Deep-Dive (Salaried Questions)

  **Answer:** Large MNC

  **Answer:** 0% (100% guaranteed base pay, no bonus haircut needed)

---

### Step-by-Step Math Breakdown

1. **Sustainable Monthly Income:**  
   Her salary is 100% fixed base pay. Sustainable Income = **₹1,10,000/month**.
2. **Current Fixed Bills:**  
   ₹14,000 (existing EMI) + ₹28,000 (rent) + ₹20,000 (groceries/utilities) = **₹62,000/month**.
3. **10% Rainy-Day Cushion:**  
   10% of ₹1,10,000 = **₹11,000/month** set aside for unexpected costs.
4. **Actual Spare Cash Left:**  
   ₹1,10,000 - ₹62,000 - ₹11,000 = **₹37,000/month**.
5. **FOIR Rule Check:**  
   For people earning over ₹1 Lakh, the maximum allowed debt ratio is 60%.  
   60% of ₹1,10,000 = ₹66,000.  
   Minus her existing ₹14,000 EMI gives **₹52,000/month**.
6. **Borrower Safe EMI Ceiling:**  
   We pick the lower of the two numbers: min(₹52,000, ₹37,000) = **₹37,000/month**.  
   *Even though the percentage rule allows ₹52,000, her Bengaluru rent leaves her with ₹37,000 in real cash. So ₹37,000 is her safe ceiling.*
7. **EMI for Requested Loan:**  
   An ₹8 Lakh loan at a fair rate of ~15.4% over 3 years costs **₹27,879/month**.  
   Since ₹27,879 is comfortably below her ₹37,000 ceiling, this loan is completely safe.

---

### 1. The Verdict: BORROW
- **Recommendation:** **`BORROW`**
- **Why:** Priya's requested EMI of ₹27,879 is well below her safe limit of ₹37,000. Her total debt will only consume 38% of her income, well inside the safe 60% limit. She also has ₹3 Lakh in savings for emergencies.
- **Confidence Rating:** **HIGH** (Known 780 CIBIL score, 5 years at an MNC, stable salary).

### 2. Lender Amount vs Safe Amount
- **What Priya Asked For:** ₹8,00,000 (₹8.0 Lakh)
- **What Priya Can Safely Afford:** **₹10,60,000 (₹10.6 Lakh)**
- **What the Bank Will Offer Her:** **₹16,50,000 (₹16.5 Lakh)**

> **Advice for Priya:** Because of your high salary, banks will try to push a loan of up to ₹16.5 Lakh on you. Do not take the bait. Stick to the ₹8 Lakh you actually need.

### 3. Fair Interest Rate & True APR
- **Fair Interest Rate Band:** **9.25% – 21.50%** per year.
  - *Target for Priya:* Given her 780 CIBIL and 5-year MNC vintage, she should push the bank for an offer between **10.5% and 11.5%**.
- **True All-In APR:** **10.89% – 23.27%**
- **Upfront Processing Fee:** 2.0% + 18% GST = ₹18,880 deducted from the loan disbursal.

### 4. EMI Ceiling, Best Tenure & Stress Test
- **Safe EMI Ceiling:** **₹37,000 / month**
- **Requested Loan EMI:** **₹27,879 / month**
- **Recommended Tenure:** **3 Years** (She could stretch to 5 years, but 3 years saves her ₹1,47,000 in total interest).
- **Stress Test (What if salary drops by 15% to ₹93,500?):**  
  - **Result: PASS (Safe)**  
  - Even if her salary drops by 15%, she will still have **₹3,621 in spare cash** every month after paying all bills, rent, and loan EMIs.

## 👨 Case 2: Ravi — Small Business Owner in Mysuru

### The Story:
Ravi is 42 years old and has run a retail grocery shop in Mysuru for 14 years. He claims typical cash earnings of ₹60,000 a month, but his official Income Tax Return (ITR) declares ₹4,20,000 a year (₹35,000 a month). He owns his commercial shop building outright, valued at ₹45,00,000. He wants to borrow ₹15,00,000 as an unsecured personal loan for 5 years to expand his store inventory. He does not know his CIBIL score.

---

### Questions Asked & Answers Given

#### Step 1: Profile & Income
- **Question:** How old are you?  
  **Answer:** 42 years
- **Question:** Which city do you live in?  
  **Answer:** Mysuru
- **Question:** What is your work type?  
  **Answer:** Self-Employed (Retail Store Owner)
- **Question:** What is your typical monthly income?  
  **Answer:** ₹60,000 (normal cash intake)
- **Question:** What is your income during a bad month (lean season)?  
  **Answer:** ₹40,000
- **Question:** What is your income during a peak month (festivals)?  
  **Answer:** ₹80,000
- **Question:** How stable is your income?  
  **Answer:** Mostly stable with seasonal swings

#### Step 2: Loan Requirements
- **Question:** What is the loan purpose?  
  **Answer:** Business expansion
- **Question:** Which loan type are you applying for?  
  **Answer:** Personal Loan (unsecured)
- **Question:** How much money do you want to borrow?  
  **Answer:** ₹15,00,000 (₹15 Lakh)
- **Question:** Over how many years do you want to repay?  
  **Answer:** 5 Years (60 months)

#### Step 3: Current Monthly Expenses & Loans
- **Question:** How much do you currently pay each month for existing loans?  
  **Answer:** ₹0 (no active debt)
- **Question:** What are your monthly household living expenses?  
  **Answer:** ₹25,000
- **Question:** How much do you pay in rent?  
  **Answer:** ₹0 (he owns both his home and his commercial shop)
- **Question:** How many family members depend on your income?  
  **Answer:** 2 (two school-going children)
- **Question:** How much emergency savings do you have?  
  **Answer:** ₹1,00,000

#### Step 4: Business Deep-Dive (Self-Employed Questions)
- **Question:** How many years have you operated this business?  
  **Answer:** 14 years (established vintage)
- **Question:** What annual income did you declare on your latest Income Tax Return (ITR)?  
  **Answer:** ₹4,20,000 (equivalent to ₹35,000/month officially)
- **Question:** What is the market value of your property or commercial shop?  
  **Answer:** ₹45,00,000 (Owns a ₹45 Lakh commercial property)
- **Question:** What is the trend of your business?  
  **Answer:** Stable
- **Question:** Does anyone else in the household earn?  
  **Answer:** ₹18,000/month (wife's tailoring/tuition income)
- **Question:** Any bounced loan payments or cheques in the last 6 months?  
  **Answer:** No

#### Step 5: Credit History
- **Question:** Do you know your credit score?  
  **Answer:** Don't Know / No (never checked)

---

### Step-by-Step Math Breakdown

1. **Sustainable Monthly Income:**  
   Ravi's tax papers show ₹35,000/mo, his typical cash is ₹60,000/mo, and a slow month is ₹40,000/mo.  
   We calculate: `(60% × ₹35,000) + (25% × ₹60,000) + (15% × ₹40,000)` = ₹21,000 + ₹15,000 + ₹6,000 = **₹42,000/month**.  
   *We do not take his claimed ₹60,000 or peak ₹80,000 because tax papers only prove ₹35,000.*
2. **Current Household Bills:**  
   ₹25,000 (living expenses for family of 4). Zero rent and zero existing loans.
3. **10% Rainy-Day Cushion:**  
   10% of ₹42,000 = **₹4,200/month**.
4. **Actual Spare Cash Left:**  
   ₹42,000 - ₹25,000 - ₹4,200 = **₹12,800/month**.
5. **FOIR Rule Check:**  
   For self-employed earning under ₹50,000, safe debt limit is 40%.  
   40% of ₹42,000 = **₹16,800/month**.
6. **Borrower Safe EMI Ceiling:**  
   min(₹16,800, ₹12,800) = **₹12,800/month**.
7. **The Shocking Problem with His Loan Request:**  
   A ₹15 Lakh unsecured personal loan at ~19.6% over 5 years costs **₹39,428/month** in EMI!  
   Ravi only has ₹12,800 in spare cash. The requested EMI is **three times higher** than his safe capacity! If he took this loan, the EMI would swallow 94% of his real earnings.

---

### 1. The Verdict: BORROW LESS
- **Recommendation:** **`BORROW LESS`**
- **Why:** Ravi's requested loan of ₹15 Lakh is far too big for his verifiable income. His safe loan capacity is **₹4,90,000**.
- **Confidence Rating:** **MEDIUM** (ITR is verified, but credit score is unknown and cash flow swings).

### 2. Lender Amount vs Safe Amount
- **What Ravi Asked For:** ₹15,00,000 (₹15.0 Lakh)
- **What Ravi Can Safely Afford:** **₹4,90,000 (₹4.9 Lakh)**
- **What the Bank Will Offer Him:** **₹7,30,000 (₹7.3 Lakh)** (Bank bases it strictly on his ₹35,000 ITR).

### 3. Fair Interest Rate & True APR
- **Fair Interest Rate Band (Personal Loan):** **11.75% – 27.50%**
- **True All-In APR:** **12.80% – 28.73%**

### 4. The Smart Move: Switch to a Secured Loan (LAP)
> **💡 Key Finding for Ravi:**  
> Ravi is asking for an **unsecured personal loan at 12%–27%**, even though he owns a commercial shop worth **₹45 Lakh** with no loans on it!  
> If he takes a **Loan Against Property (LAP)** instead:
> - The interest rate drops to **9.0% – 12.0%** (saving him over ₹4,00,000 in interest).
> - The loan tenure can be stretched to 7–10 years, cutting his monthly EMI by more than half.

- **Safe EMI Ceiling:** **₹12,800 / month**
- **Recommended Tenure:** **7 Years** (for a safe ₹4.9 Lakh loan, keeping the EMI at ₹10,771).
- **Stress Test (Income drops by 15%):**  
  - **Result on ₹15L loan: SEVERE FAIL** (Monthly deficit of -₹28,728).  
  - **Result on ₹4.9L loan: PASS** (Manageable).

### Step-by-Step Math Breakdown

1. **Sustainable Monthly Income:**  
   Conservative blend: `(60% × ₹26,000 lowest month) + (40% × ₹28,000 typical month)` = ₹15,600 + ₹11,200 = **₹26,800/month**.
2. **Current Fixed Expenses:**  
   ₹5,000 (existing EMI) + ₹6,000 (rent) + ₹18,000 (household living) = **₹29,000/month**.
3. **The Harsh Reality:**  
   Anita's basic monthly survival costs (₹29,000) **already exceed her sustainable income (₹26,800) by ₹2,200 every single month!**  
   She is currently surviving by taking out new instant app loans at 30% interest to pay for daily food.
4. **Borrower Safe EMI Ceiling:**  
   Spare cash = ₹26,800 - ₹29,000 = -₹2,200.  
   Since spare cash is negative, her Safe EMI Capacity is strictly **₹0 / month**.
5. **The Proposed New Loan:**  
   A ₹1.5 Lakh bike loan over 3 years would add an EMI of **₹5,651/month**.  
   Adding ₹5,651 to a household already short on cash would lead to immediate loan default, missed rent, and harassment from lenders.

---

### 1. The Verdict: DON'T BORROW
- **Recommendation:** **`DONT_BORROW`**
- **Why:** Anita has zero capacity for new debt. Her existing living costs already exceed her income, she already bounced a payment last month, and she is trapped under 3 payday app loans charging over 30% interest.
- **Confidence Rating:** **LOW** (High financial stress, variable income, recent payment failure).

### 2. Lender Amount vs Safe Amount
- **What Anita Asked For:** ₹1,50,000 (₹1.5 Lakh)
- **What Anita Can Safely Afford:** **₹0 (Zero)**
- **What a High-Risk Subprime Lender Might Offer:** ₹30,000 (token high-interest loan)

> **⚠️ Urgent Warning for Anita:** Do not let a two-wheeler showroom agent or a fintech app persuade you to sign up for this loan. You will not be able to pay month one.

- *Her recent bounce and multiple app loans push any new borrowing toward predatory 28% territory.*
